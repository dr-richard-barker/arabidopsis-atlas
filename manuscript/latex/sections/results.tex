\subsection{Real spaceflight response, by organ}

Of the five organs modelled, one (root) has a directly tissue-matched real spaceflight
comparison: OSD-120 profiled root tissue specifically, at Day 13, under
\NumFlightSamplesOsdOneTwentyZero{} flight and \NumGroundSamplesOsdOneTwentyZero{} ground
control samples across \NumGenesOsdOneTwentyZero{} genes. The largest mean flight/ground
CPM ratio in that comparison is \TopFoldChangeOsdOneTwentyZero{} $\log_2$ units, for
\texttt{\TopGeneOsdOneTwentyZero{}}.

OSD-314 profiled whole seedlings, not a single organ, across the same
\NumGenesOsdThreeFourteen{} genes, comparing \NumMicrogSamplesOsdThreeFourteen{} real
microgravity samples against \NumGroundSamplesOsdThreeFourteen{} 1g ground samples; its
largest ratio is \TopFoldChangeOsdThreeFourteen{} $\log_2$ units, for
\texttt{\TopGeneOsdThreeFourteen{}}. Because it is not organ-specific, we report it in the
data provenance ledger rather than attach it to any single organ in the viewer, to avoid
implying a tissue-level claim the data does not support.

The remaining three organs (rosette leaf, inflorescence axis, flower/silique) have no
organ-matched spaceflight dataset wired into this version of the atlas; the viewer states
this plainly for each rather than substituting the whole-seedling numbers as if they were
organ-specific.

\subsection{Realistic geometry and real ecotype shape-morphing}

Inspecting rice-atlas's actual source (not just its file names) showed that its visual
quality comes mainly from spline-swept tube construction -- a Catmull-Rom spline plus a
parallel-transport (rotation-minimizing) frame builder -- rather than from Blender
sculpting, which it uses only for a thin Solidify-modifier pass on leaf blades. We
re-implemented the same class of technique independently in the viewer itself (a
double-reflection rotation-minimizing frame builder, \texttt{app/src/organGeometry/}),
replacing the original primitive-shape organs with tapering, curved tube geometry and a
parametric curved leaf-blade surface.

We then wired this geometry to real per-ecotype trait data so a viewer can select a
natural accession and see the plant's shape actually respond. \NumEcotypes{} ecotypes are
available; \NumMagicFounderEcotypesUsed{} of them (Col-0, Ler, Ws, Tsu-0, Edi-0) have real,
directly-computed rosette-shape descriptors from Camargo et al.\ \citeyearpar{Camargo2014}'s
own published raw data (\NRosetteImagesPerEcotype{} images per accession): mean rosette
Compactness is \ColZeroCompactness{} for Col-0, \LerCompactness{} for Ler, and
\WsCompactness{} for Ws -- \MostCompactEcotype{} is the most compact and
\LeastCompactEcotype{} the least compact of the five measured here, independently
corroborating Ler's known compact habit with an entirely different, image-based method
than the genetic literature that first described it. Ler's short pedicels and blunt
siliques reflect the real, natural \textit{erecta} (\textit{er}) mutation
\citep{Torii1996}; a quantitative proxy from \citet{Bundy2012} gives a mature wild-type
pedicel of \PedicelWildTypeMm{}mm versus a $\sim$\PedicelErShorteningFold{}$\times$ shorter
induced \textit{er-105} pedicel, disclosed in the application itself as a proxy from an
induced allele in a Columbia background, not a direct field measurement of natural Ler.
Cvi-0, absent from the MAGIC founder panel, instead varies by a real, directly-quoted
comparison from \citet{Coneva2018}: \CviExtraLeavesVsLer{} more leaves than Ler in
long-day conditions, with thicker leaf blades.

Every gap is disclosed rather than filled with a plausible value: Cvi-0's
rosette-compactness parameter defaults to the Col-0 value because no measurement exists
for it in the dataset used; ERECTA status for Cvi-0/Tsu-0/Edi-0 is stated as an assumed
default, not confirmed per-accession; and no accession-labelled reference photo was found
for Ws, so its photo link is simply absent rather than substituted.

\subsection{Verification of the viewer itself}

The viewer was checked interactively, not only by static code review: in both a local
development server and the actual production build served at the live GitHub Pages URL,
clicking each organ was confirmed to open an information panel showing the correct TraVA
category, the correct geometry citation, and -- for root -- the real gene table described
above, with no console errors in either environment.
